# Jet-pump installation — alpha.28

## Building and servicing

Place complete assemblies within the two-block band at the interior perimeter,
at any elevation where the entire six-block assembly fits inside the vessel.
Opposite assemblies still need matching rows/elevations and opposite facing.
The tall, six-cell construction models remain visible while the reactor is unformed.
Alpha.28 recognizes raised construction roots and caps modeled active fuel at
nine blocks. See [the 17×17×28 fit update](REACTOR-FIT-UPDATE.md).

Once the reactor forms, the renderer fits the installed pump models into the
annular gap **outside the core shroud and inside the vessel wall**. Their upper
ends remain below the modeled core top; their lower ends reach the lower support
region. The arrangement fits the vessel footprint and pump count. Only complete pumps recognized by the existing placement
rules receive the installed appearance. Unmatched pumps can still be installed
visually, but gain no paired-flow credit.

The original blocks stay saved in their construction positions. While formed,
these hidden columns have no collision or selection boxes, so they do not obstruct
entry through an open head. Unform the vessel to access or remove the pumps at
their original positions. Merely opening the head leaves the reactor formed and
the pumps in their installed appearance. Reloading or repairing a vessel preserves
the pump count and flow behavior. Old two-column pump saves remain supported.

## Geometry and performance

Installed pumps now use four original Blender-authored parts: the lower diffuser
and riser bend, plain straight tubes, upper inlet assembly, and restrainer brace.
All fittings receive the same scale on every axis. Only the straight tubes have
an independently adjusted length, bounded to 1.4–3.4 model units. The full pump
is therefore 4–6 units tall before uniform scaling, rather than stretching with
vessel height indefinitely. Small vessels scale down the entire assembly; tall
vessels leave more space above it. This deliberately favors believable proportions
over holding the inlet at a fixed fraction of an arbitrarily tall modeled core.

Sources and the current Blender fit review are in
`art/models/reactor_vessel/installed_jet/`, generated by `build_installed_jets.py`.
`tools-export-installed-jets.py --check` verifies the exported assets and their
fit bounds. Alpha.24's `review_jet_installation.py` and `installed_jets.blend`
remain historical references for the earlier stretched appearance.
No new per-pump entity or renderer is added.
Installed geometry is combined into a cached GPU mesh and drawn only when the
reactor interior is visible. Placement/collision indices update on structure and
chunk lifecycle changes; rendering does not scan the vessel or rebuild pipe graphs.

This is a game-scale placement approximation, not an engineering dimension model.
It does not change hydraulic capacity, fuel counts, or
the ten jet assemblies supported by each external recirculation pump.

## References

- [GE BWR/6 General Description](https://corephysics.com/docs/BWR6GeneralDescription.pdf):
  jet pumps in the downcomer between the shroud and vessel, discharging into the
  lower core plenum.
- [NRC Issue 12: BWR Jet Pump Integrity](https://www.nrc.gov/sr0933/section-3-new-generic-issues/issue-12-bwr-jet-pump-integrity):
  inlet elevation approximately two-thirds of core height. The game model uses
  this as an approximate visual reference, rather than a plant-specific measurement.

## Verification

Earlier GameTests cover formation/unformation, save/reload, legacy cells, invalid mounting,
removing an assembly, and matching/flow counts. The client harness checks live
construction visibility, collision state, materials and static-mesh reuse, plus
shroud/wall/inter-pump clearance at minimum, reference, maximum, rectangular and
tall vessel sizes with several installed counts. Those were previous-release
checks. Alpha.28 verification intentionally uses only the requested 17×17×28
fixture, including raised roots, a full square drive grid, nine lifecycle/views,
actual baked vertex containment and the runtime-derived Blender review.
