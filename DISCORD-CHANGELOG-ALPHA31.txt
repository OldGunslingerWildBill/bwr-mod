Realistic BWR 0.1.0-alpha.31 — Buildable containment

• Build a sealed containment room around your reactor and suppression pool. The controller reports leaks, free volume, weakest spot and pressure rating.
• Add rated steam/water/power/data penetrations and powered isolation valves. Open process lines can bypass containment.
• Install the Blender-modeled vault door or two-leaf airlock. Redstone cycles the interlocked leaves; CC can force both open at a leak cost.
• Power a nitrogen supply with canisters. The wetwell vent works with both concrete basins and enclosed suppression tanks when its outlet reaches exterior air.
• CC:Tweaked now reads the room, openings, atmosphere and bypass flow, and can isolate penetrations. The controller status readout includes hydrogen, oxygen and pressure; its GUI comes in a later overhaul.
• Fixed persistent-world tank drop-test readiness and added containment regressions.
