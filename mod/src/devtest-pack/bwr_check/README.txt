Datapack used only by :mod:runWorldgenCheck (copied into run/worldgenCheck/world/datapacks by build.gradle). Not part of the mod jar.
One flat dimension per uranium biome group (flat_arid, ...): bedrock, 63 deepslate, 80 stone, 3 dirt (surface at y 82), the group biome, normal biome decoration.
And one per thorium biome group (flat_th_highland, _placer, _lowland, _ocean): the same, with 3 sand on top in the placer one (the monazite sand placers replace sand).
